DIGSHI

UX RESEARCH & INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE

Digshi

An AI-powered voice assistant concept for people with low digital confidence.

Inspired by the Sanskrit word Digdarshi, meaning one who shows the way, Digshi represents a vision of making technology more accessible and inclusive.

I envisioned Digshi for people who struggle with digital applications because of unfamiliar interfaces, limited digital confidence or difficulty navigating smartphone tasks.

The idea is to use voice-based AI assistance to help people navigate everyday digital experiences with greater confidence and independence.

The Problem

  • Difficulty navigating Ola and Uber.
  • Confusion while using food delivery applications.
  • Fear and hesitation around mobile banking.
  • Dependence on others for simple digital tasks.

My Research Process

  • Google Forms and user interviews
  • User personas
  • Empathy mapping
  • How Might We questions
  • Competitor analysis
  • Information Architecture in FigJam
